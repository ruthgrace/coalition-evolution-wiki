# Coalition Evolution: A Literature Review

An automated literature review exploring how coalitions of power form, shift, and dissolve over time, and what research exists on historical and contemporary examples of these dynamics.

Built with [Astro](https://astro.build/) + [Starlight](https://starlight.astro.build/).

## Topic

How do coalitions of power change over time? This review surveys academic research on:

- Formation and dissolution of political, social, and economic coalitions
- Theoretical frameworks for understanding coalition dynamics (e.g., game theory, institutional analysis, network theory)
- Historical case studies of shifting power alliances
- Factors that drive coalition stability and collapse
